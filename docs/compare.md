# Sukhna: reference photos vs the game

Phase 8 verification (plan §8). Every view below was rendered in the Claude
Desktop Browser pane with `window.__shot` (1200×675, high tier), from fixed
camera spots chosen to match a reference photo in [`reference/`](../reference).
Each spot is shot at **pre-dawn** (06:55), **sunrise** (07:19), **bright
morning** (09:15) and in **fog**; r8 also at **sunset** (17:45), since the
photo is an evening one. The overview is set against the aerial r9.

The camera spots are recorded at the end. Reference photos r3, r4 and r5 are
AI-generated or heavily stylised (plan §2), so they set the mood only and
aren't compared.

## r2: the bright winter promenade (the key reference)

| Reference | Pre-dawn | Sunrise | Bright morning | Fog |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ![r2](../reference/Sukhna_Lake_in_Chandigarh.jpg) | ![](shots/cmp-r2-predawn.jpg) | ![](shots/cmp-r2-sunrise.jpg) | ![](shots/cmp-r2-bright.jpg) | ![](shots/cmp-r2-fog.jpg) |

**Matches:** the pale-grey walk with no kerb paint; the low, flat-topped river-cobble parapet on the lake edge (no railing); royal palms and dense broadleaf trees on the land side; the walk curving away toward the boat club and the far shore as a low treeline; a clear pale-blue winter sky fading to a pale horizon; people walking in pairs.

**Doesn't match yet:**
- The embankment. In r2 a wide grassed slope of stone pitching falls from the parapet to a band of cream boulders at the water. Here, at s ≈ 1960, the real OSM shoreline comes almost to the parapet, so the slope is only a metre or two wide; where the lake stands further off, the slope and its drawdown band appear.
- The water. r2's lake is glassy and carries long reflections of the treeline; ours is steel grey-mauve with a Fresnel sky tint and wind ripples, but no reflections.
- Cloud: r2 has none; the cel clouds are always present (fewer in bright weather would read truer).
- The boat club at the end of the view is smaller and further than in r2 (the real distance; r2 is a phone camera with a wide lens).

**Matches:** the composition (walk left, parapet, the dam curving away with the lake on the right, the treeline across), the warm low-sun palette on the water, crowds along the walk, silhouetted palms.

**Doesn't match yet:**

## r1: the garden end at sunrise

| Reference | Pre-dawn | Sunrise | Bright | Fog |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ![r1](../reference/23913875383_6fd6bd16ef_b.jpg) | ![](shots/cmp-r1-predawn.jpg) | ![](shots/cmp-r1-sunrise.jpg) | ![](shots/cmp-r1-bright.jpg) | ![](shots/cmp-r1-fog.jpg) |

**Matches:** the orange sunrise sky, the lamps glowing before sunrise and out after it, the parapet and the walk with a lone walker, silhouetted trees.

**Doesn't match:**
- In r1 the sun rises over the lake beyond the garden. With the real geometry the lake at the garden (east) end lies to the NNW and the January sun rises at 114° (ESE), behind the camera. So either r1 was taken elsewhere on the shore, or it is a sunset; the game keeps the real sun.
- r1's garden has a thatched gazebo, terracotta pavers, flower beds, a low chain-link fence, a blue signboard and carved stone seats. The game's garden at this end has lawns, paths and trees; the gazebo, pavers and signboard exist at the plaza (west end), not here.

## r7: the boat club jetty

| Reference | Pre-dawn | Sunrise | Bright | Fog |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ![r7](../reference/sukhna-lake%20(1).jpg) | ![](shots/cmp-r7-predawn.jpg) | ![](shots/cmp-r7-sunrise.jpg) | ![](shots/cmp-r7-bright.jpg) | ![](shots/cmp-r7-fog.jpg) |

**Matches:** looking out from the jetty to the island (OSM's largest islet) with its pavilion and trees, the pink launch at the jetty, a swan boat, the hills beyond.

**Doesn't match yet:**
- r7's foreground is a long row of moored pedal boats (blue, yellow, red, with swan and dragon heads). The game moors them along the jetty's sides, so from its end they are mostly behind the camera.
- The water in r7 mirrors the island; ours doesn't reflect.
- r7 is shot from the paved jetty edge with the pavers in the foreground; this spot stands at the jetty's end looking outward, so the grey-blue pavers with the pink border (built, r7) are out of frame.
